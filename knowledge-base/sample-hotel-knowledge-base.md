## 1. Hotel Overview

Grandview Hotel is a modern boutique hotel located in the heart of **Riverside District**, a lively urban neighborhood known for its restaurants, shopping, business offices, and relaxed evening atmosphere. The hotel combines contemporary comfort with personalized service and is designed for business travelers, couples, families, and short-stay guests looking for a comfortable 3.5 to 4 star experience.

## 2. Room Types

### Standard Room: $60/night

The Standard Room is a comfortable and practical option for solo travelers or couples looking for an affordable stay without sacrificing essential amenities.

- **Bed:** 1 Queen-size bed
- **Size:** Approximately 24 m²
- **Sleeps:** Up to 2 guests
- **Features:** High-speed Wi-Fi, smart TV, air conditioning, work desk, wardrobe, mini refrigerator, tea and coffee station, electronic safe, and private bathroom with walk-in shower.
- **Best for:** Solo travelers, couples, business travelers, and short stays.

### Deluxe Room: $90/night

The Deluxe Room provides additional space and upgraded comfort, making it suitable for guests who want a little more room to relax or work during their stay.

- **Bed:** 1 King-size bed
- **Size:** Approximately 30 m²
- **Sleeps:** Up to 2 guests
- **Features:** High-speed Wi-Fi, smart TV, air conditioning, larger work desk, seating area, mini refrigerator, tea and coffee station, electronic safe, bathrobe, slippers, and private bathroom with walk-in shower.
- **Best for:** Couples, business travelers, and guests staying for several nights.

### Suite: $150/night

The Suite offers a separate living area and bedroom, providing extra privacy and space for guests who want a more premium hotel experience.

- **Bed:** 1 King-size bed
- **Size:** Approximately 48 m²
- **Sleeps:** Up to 2 adults
- **Features:** Separate bedroom and living room, sofa, dining table, high-speed Wi-Fi, two smart TVs, air conditioning, mini refrigerator, coffee machine, electronic safe, bathrobe and slippers, premium toiletries, and spacious bathroom with walk-in shower and soaking tub.
- **Best for:** Couples, business executives, long-stay guests, and guests celebrating special occasions.

### Family Room: $120/night

The Family Room is designed to give families enough sleeping and living space while keeping everyone together in one room.

- **Beds:** 1 King-size bed + 2 Single beds
- **Size:** Approximately 40 m²
- **Sleeps:** Up to 4 guests
- **Features:** High-speed Wi-Fi, smart TV, air conditioning, seating area, work desk, mini refrigerator, tea and coffee station, electronic safe, children's amenities upon request, and private bathroom with walk-in shower.
- **Best for:** Families with children, small groups, and guests traveling together.

## 4. Hotel Policies

### Check-In and Check-Out

- **Check-in:** From 2:00 PM
- **Check-out:** By 11:00 AM
- Guests arriving before 2:00 PM may request early check-in. Early check-in is subject to room availability and may involve an additional fee.
- Guests who need to leave later can request late check-out. Late check-out is subject to availability and may incur an additional charge.

### Cancellation Policy

Guests may cancel their reservation **free of charge up to 48 hours before the scheduled check-in time**.

Cancellations made less than 48 hours before check-in will be charged **one night's room rate**. No-shows are also subject to a charge of one night's room rate unless otherwise stated in the booking confirmation.

### Pet Policy

Small pets may be accepted in selected rooms with prior approval from the hotel. A pet fee may apply. Guests must notify the hotel before arrival so an appropriate room can be assigned. Service animals are accommodated in accordance with applicable requirements.

### Smoking Policy

Grandview Hotel is a **non-smoking hotel**. Smoking and vaping are not permitted inside guest rooms, hallways, restaurants, or other indoor areas. Designated outdoor smoking areas are available. A cleaning fee may be charged if smoking is detected inside a guest room.

### Extra Guests and Children

- Children are welcome at Grandview Hotel.
- Children under 6 years old may stay free when using existing bedding.
- Children aged 6 to 12 may be accommodated for an additional fee where the room configuration permits.
- Guests aged 13 and above are charged according to the applicable extra-guest rate.
- Extra beds and baby cots are subject to availability and should be requested in advance.
- Maximum occupancy depends on the selected room type.

### Payment Methods

The hotel accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, local debit cards, bank transfer for eligible bookings, and cash in the hotel's accepted local currency. A valid payment method may be required to guarantee a reservation.

### ID Requirements

All guests must present a **valid government-issued photo ID or passport** at check-in. International guests should present their passport and any required travel documentation. The name on the identification document should match the reservation details.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I check in early?**
Early check-in is available depending on room availability. The standard check-in time is 2:00 PM. If your room is ready earlier, the front desk can check you in without waiting. Guaranteed early check-in may be available for an additional fee.

**Can I check out late?**
Late check-out can be requested through the front desk. It depends on availability and may involve an additional charge. Please contact reception as early as possible on the day of departure.

**Does the hotel provide an airport shuttle?**
Yes. Grandview Hotel can arrange airport transfers through its transportation partners. Airport transfers should be requested in advance, preferably at least 24 hours before arrival. The front desk can provide the current transfer rate and pickup instructions.

**Is breakfast included?**
Breakfast is included with selected room rates and packages. If breakfast is not included in your reservation, it can be purchased separately at the Riverside Restaurant. Breakfast is served from 6:30 AM to 10:00 AM.

**Do you offer room service?**
Yes. Room service is available daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Guests can order selected breakfast items, meals, snacks, and drinks from the in-room dining menu.

**How do I get the Wi-Fi password?**
Complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel. Guests receive the network name and access instructions at check-in. If you need assistance connecting, contact the front desk and a staff member can help.

**Do you have accessible rooms?**
Yes. The hotel has selected accessible rooms with wider doorways, accessible bathrooms, and other accessibility features. Availability should be confirmed before booking so the appropriate room can be reserved.

**Can I book multiple rooms for a group?**
Yes. Group reservations are available for guests booking multiple rooms or traveling for an event. For larger groups, contact the front desk or reservations team so they can provide availability and group rates.

**Can the hotel accommodate dietary restrictions?**
Yes. The restaurant can accommodate several dietary requirements, including vegetarian, vegan, and selected allergy-related requests. Please inform the hotel when making your reservation or speak with the restaurant team before ordering so suitable options can be confirmed.

**Is parking free?**
Yes. Complimentary on-site parking is available for hotel guests. Spaces are subject to availability and are provided on a first-come, first-served basis.

**Can I request a specific type of bed?**
Yes. Guests can request their preferred bed configuration when making a reservation. Requests are subject to room availability and cannot always be guaranteed.

**Can I store my luggage before check-in or after check-out?**
Yes. The front desk can store luggage securely for hotel guests before check-in or after check-out at no additional charge.

**Do you have a swimming pool?**
Yes. The hotel has a rooftop outdoor swimming pool. It is open daily from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

**Is there a gym?**
Yes. The fitness center is available to hotel guests 24 hours a day using room-key access.

**Can I arrange a birthday or special occasion setup?**
Yes. The hotel can arrange selected celebration extras such as flowers, cake, room decorations, or a special dinner setup. Requests should be made in advance so the concierge team can confirm availability and pricing.
